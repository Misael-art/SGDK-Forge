Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: original ability-hat and visual-effect concept sheet for a non-commercial Mega Drive platformer; source reference only, never final sprites.
Primary request: A clean 1024x512 pixel-art production sheet organized in five equal columns for five distinct powers. In every column show (1) an isolated hat/adornment, (2) that hat resting on the head of a small light-gray spherical hero silhouette used only as scale/placement guide, and (3) a compact 2-to-3 key-frame visual effect shape. The five original designs: FIRE—flaming crown/tiara plus an orange-yellow flame jet; BEAM—tall pointed wizard hat with a star plus angular yellow electric arc; CUTTER—headband with a curved blade plus silver boomerang blade; STONE—no hat, instead show the spherical hero transformed into a clearly rock-like gray stone form plus dust puff; SWORD—green pointed cap plus a white-cyan arcing slash.
Scene/backdrop: solid #ff00ff magenta chroma-key background, no ground/no shadows.
Style/medium: original hand-authored Mega Drive 16-bit pixel-art reference sheet, hard nearest-neighbor edges, clear chunky clusters, no copied Nintendo/Kirby art or branding.
Composition/framing: 5-column comparison, each power extremely distinguishable by shape as well as color; hats fit a future 20x12 pixel scale and effects are compact sprite forms.
Lighting/mood: friendly, playful, immediately readable.
Color palette: at most 15 opaque RGB333-grid colors per ability plus magenta; all channel values must be 0,36,73,109,146,182,219,255. Every effect uses at most 6 colors; flat colors only.
Constraints: no alpha, no antialiasing, no gradients, no airbrushing, no logos/watermarks, no detailed text. Stone remains recognizable as a spherical hero via silhouette.
Avoid: repeated same hat silhouette, a fifth traditional hat for stone, diffuse explosions, rendered illustrations.
