# Prompt 01 — TAÍNA model sheet (heroína)

Salvar em: `data/source_art/concept/taina_model_sheet/`
Escopo: concept_art (referência para lineart 1px; NÃO é sprite final)

## Quem é ela (contexto para você julgar as saídas)

Taína, ex-instrutora de muay thai da vila dos pescadores de Porto Bravo, ~28 anos,
brasileira parda, físico atlético funcional (não fisiculturista), postura de guarda
alta de muay thai. Roupa de treino de quem trabalha: top esportivo desbotado
laranja-queimado, calça leve de treino escura com faixa amarrada na cintura,
bandagem nas mãos e canelas, descalça ou com sapatilha fina. Expressão: firme,
cansada de esperar — sem pose de pin-up, sem sensualização.

## Prompt A — turnaround (gerar primeiro)

```
Character model sheet, full-body turnaround of a Brazilian female muay thai
fighter from a 1990s coastal working-class town: front view, 3/4 view, side
view, back view, standing in relaxed guard. Athletic functional build,
brown skin, dark curly hair tied short, faded burnt-orange sports top, dark
loose training pants with a waist sash, hand and shin wraps, barefoot.
Angular arcade-fighter anatomy with sharp cel-shadow muscle planes, clean
bold outlines, flat cel shading in exactly 3 tones per material, hue-shifted
shadows (warm light #F2C29A skin, deep cool shadow #2E1F3A, accent #FF5533),
strong readable silhouette, proportions 3.5 heads tall (stocky arcade
proportion, NOT realistic 7-head proportion), neutral flat light-gray
background, no text, evenly spaced views on one sheet.
```

Parâmetros sugeridos: 2048x1152 (16:9) ou maior; stylize/creativity médio;
4 variações.

## Prompt B — key poses de ação (depois do turnaround aprovado)

```
Action pose sheet of the same Brazilian female muay thai fighter (athletic,
3.5 heads tall, burnt-orange top, dark pants with sash, hand wraps), nine
poses arranged in a grid, each pose fully visible and separated: 1) idle
high guard with subtle breathing stance, 2) walking stride, 3) left jab
fully extended, 4) right cross with hip rotation, 5) low roundhouse kick to
the shin, 6) flying knee strike, 7) two-handed push-shove with full body
weight forward (signature move), 8) flinching from a hit, 9) knocked down
on the ground. Angular arcade-fighter anatomy, sharp cel-shadow planes,
bold clean outlines, flat 3-tone cel shading with hue-shifted shadows,
strong silhouettes readable as pure black shapes, muay thai technique
accuracy (elbows in, chin down), neutral flat background, no text.
```

Nota: a pose 7 é o **Empurrão de Maré** — o golpe assinatura. Se ela não parecer
um empurrão pesado com o corpo inteiro, regere só esta pose num prompt isolado.

## Prompt C — cabeça/expressões (para o retrato de HUD 16x16)

```
Close-up expression sheet of the same fighter's face: neutral focused,
gritted-teeth effort, pain wince, small confident smile. Angular planes,
bold outline, flat 3-tone cel shading, dark curly hair, brown skin
(#F2C29A highlight tone), readable at small size, neutral background.
```
