# Prompt 01 — TAÍNA identity/model sheet source candidates

Salvar cada asset em:

- `data/source_art/concept/taina_identity_turnaround/`

- `data/source_art/concept/taina_face_hud_expressions/`

Escopo: `concept_art` / `source_candidate`. Referência para compressão autoral
em pixel art; NÃO é sprite final de 48x48.

Leia junto: `doc/art/authorial_line_style_contract.json`.

## Quem é ela

Taína, ex-instrutora de muay thai da vila dos pescadores de Porto Bravo, ~28
anos, brasileira parda, físico atlético funcional, postura de guarda alta.
Roupa de treino de quem trabalha: top esportivo laranja-queimado gasto, calça
leve escura, faixa amarrada na cintura, bandagem nas mãos e canelas. Expressão:
firme, cansada de esperar — sem pin-up, sem sensualização.

## Ganchos autorais obrigatórios

- guarda alta diagonal de muay thai;
- faixa lateral como cauda gráfica;
- cabelo cacheado preso em massa curta irregular;
- bandagens claras em mãos e canelas;
- sobrancelha forte, nariz em cunha, mandíbula compacta;
- mãos/punhos grandes o bastante para comunicar impacto.

Bloqueie qualquer resultado que pareça lutadora fitness genérica, fashion
fighter de 7 cabeças, rosto anime liso ou roupa glamourosa.

## Prompt A — turnaround

```
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: concept art source candidate, not final pixel art
Primary request: full-body character turnaround model sheet for TAÍNA, an
authorial Brazilian female muay thai fighter from a 1990s working-class coastal
dock town.

Subject: brown-skinned Brazilian woman, about 28, functional athletic build,
compact arcade proportions suitable for later compression into a 3.5-head 48px
Mega Drive sprite, relaxed high muay thai guard. Burnt-orange worn sports top,
dark loose training pants, asymmetric waist sash hanging to one side, hand and
shin wraps, barefoot or very thin training shoes, short tied curly hair mass.

Authorial line contract: assertive dark purple contour with variable weight,
not uniform black outline; sparse angular internal cuts; hard wedge-shaped cel
shadows; strong brow, wedge nose, compact jaw, focused tired eyes; hands and
feet deliberately graphic and readable. The sash, wraps and hair mass must be
recognizable in pure black silhouette.

Composition: front view, 3/4 view, side view, back view, evenly spaced on one
clean sheet, full body visible, neutral flat light-gray background.

Style/medium: hand-drawn 16-bit arcade concept art with hard cel shading,
sharp angular planes, 2-3 tones per material, hue-shifted shadows.

Color palette: burnt orange #FF5533, deep cool dark #2E1F3A, warm brown skin
highlight #F2C29A, muted green accent #1A6B5A.

Constraints: no text, no logos, no watermark, no photorealism, no 3D render,
no soft airbrush, no gradients, no extra limbs, no broken hands, no realistic
7-head fashion anatomy, no generic anime fighter face, no pin-up pose.
```

## Prompt B — key poses de ação

```

Subject invariants: compact arcade proportions, brown skin, short tied curly

Style/medium: hand-drawn 16-bit arcade concept art, hard cel shading, sharp
shadow planes, 2-3 tones per material, no motion blur.

Constraints: no text, no background scene, no final sprite sheet, no frame
sequence claim, no extra limbs, no generic kickboxing pose, no soft anatomy,
no pin-up framing.
```
