# Prompt 02 — CRIA e ESTIVADOR model sheets (inimigos do slice)

Salvar em: `data/source_art/concept/cria_model_sheet/` e
`data/source_art/concept/estivador_model_sheet/`
Escopo: concept_art. Os dois compartilham UMA paleta de 15 cores no jogo —
prefira saídas com famílias de cor próximas (azul #4A5C8A, vermelho #CC2244,
couro #8A6B4A, escuro #2A2A3A).

## CRIA — rusher magro (44px no jogo, telegraph de 12 frames)

```
Enemy character model sheet for a 1990s Brazilian dockside brawler game:
a lean fast street kid ("Cria"), male, late teens, wiry thin build leaning
aggressively forward, faded blue sleeveless shirt, rolled-up shorts,
flip-flops or barefoot, cheap cap worn backwards. Poses on one sheet:
1) front view standing, 2) side view standing, 3) sprinting lunge with one
arm cocked back (attack telegraph, body tilted 30 degrees forward),
4) wild haymaker punch, 5) flinching hit reaction, 6) knocked down flat.
Angular arcade anatomy, bold outlines, flat 3-tone cel shading with
hue-shifted shadows, silhouette must read as "fast and fragile" versus
heavier fighters, dominant palette blue #4A5C8A with red #CC2244 accent,
neutral flat background, no text.
```

Aceite: silhueta inclinada para frente lê como "rápido/frágil"; nada de faca ou
arma (fora de escopo); cabe em meia paleta.

## ESTIVADOR — grappler pesado (56px no jogo, telegraph de 18 frames)

```
Enemy character model sheet for the same game: a heavy dock worker
("Estivador"), male, 40s, massive square torso and thick arms, cargo work
vest over bare chest, heavy canvas pants, work boots, thick leather gloves,
rope coil on the belt. Poses on one sheet: 1) front view standing with
wide stance, 2) side view, 3) slow advancing step with both arms spread
wide open (grab telegraph, clearly readable), 4) bear-hug grab squeezing
forward, 5) overhead slam follow-through, 6) staggered hit reaction
(barely flinching, heavy poise), 7) knocked down. Angular arcade anatomy
with square mass shapes, bold outlines, flat 3-tone cel shading,
hue-shifted shadows, dominant palette leather brown #8A6B4A and dark
#2A2A3A with red #CC2244 accent, silhouette must read as "slow, heavy,
dangerous up close", neutral flat background, no text.
```

Aceite: braços abertos do telegraph legíveis em silhueta pura; massa quadrada
contrasta com a diagonal da TAÍNA e a inclinação do CRIA (teste: as 3 silhuetas
lado a lado precisam ser distinguíveis instantaneamente).

## Teste conjunto obrigatório

Antes de aprovar qualquer um: cole as três silhuetas (TAÍNA, CRIA, ESTIVADOR)
lado a lado em preto puro. Se duas se confundirem, regere o que falhou — leitura
de crowd é pilar assinatura do projeto.
