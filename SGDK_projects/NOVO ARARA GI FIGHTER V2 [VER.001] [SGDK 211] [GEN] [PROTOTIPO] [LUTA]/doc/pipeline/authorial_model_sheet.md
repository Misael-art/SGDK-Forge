# authorial_model_sheet

character_id: caio_arara
role: P1 playable fighter

## Identity

Caio Arara is a Brazilian BJJ fighter from Lapa, Rio. He is a young adult athlete with medium brown skin, short wavy black hair, strong eyebrows, a confident focused expression, a distinct nose and chin, and a technical grappler posture.

## Body And Silhouette

- wide shoulders, compact waist, low hips.
- center of gravity low, knees soft, feet bare and planted.
- hands open for grips, not fists-first karate guard.
- relaxed confidence outside strikes, explosive hip and grip motion during offense.

## Outfit

- white BJJ gi open at chest with defined lapels.
- navy rashguard under the gi.
- black belt tied low.
- small green/yellow patches, not loud branding.
- light finger tape, bare feet.

## Differentiators From Karate Fighters

- no headband.
- no orange gi.
- no straight karate horse stance.
- no closed karate chamber fists as default.
- throws and grips are core silhouette beats.

## P2 Variant Contract

Davi Arara uses the same rig/scale for versus testing, but reads as a distinct fighter: deep blue or blue-grey gi, different face marks/patch placement, slightly sharper expression, shared BJJ posture.
