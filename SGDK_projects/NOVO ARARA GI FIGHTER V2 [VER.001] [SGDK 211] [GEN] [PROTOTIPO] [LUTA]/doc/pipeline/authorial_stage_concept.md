# authorial_stage_concept

stage_id: lapa_open_mat_noite
role: first playable fighting arena

## Fantasy

An elevated BJJ open mat competition court at night in Rio. The arena feels festive and competitive without parody: blue/green tatame, low railing, silhouettes of spectators, warm event lights, flags, fictitious banners, cool skyline, and an authorial suggestion of the Arcos da Lapa in the distance.

## Plane Roles

BG_B: cold night skyline, distant arches, moonlit blue/violet mass, slow parallax.
BG_A: playable platform, tatame, rail, banners, warm lights, readable floor horizon.
SPRITES: fighters, hit spark, optional impact dust if budget allows.
WINDOW/HUD: formal lifebars, names, timer, round markers.

## Color Separation

Background stays cold and lower contrast. Tatame and warm lights define the playable plane. Caio's white gi and Davi's blue gi must remain the brightest readable silhouettes in the central play area.
