# AAA Video Curation Handoff

Status: `candidate_applied_not_verified`

Use this handoff when `cutscene-cinematic-direction` designs cutscenes, dramatic transitions, intro sequences, scripted visual scenes or cinematic FX.

## New Required Routes

- Route fades and state changes to `game-state-transition-architect`.
- Route palette effects to `palette-cram-curator` and `raster-palette-hint-director`.
- Route SFX and stingers to `sfx-prep-fm-psg-pcm`.
- Route final runtime proof to `emulator-vdp-evidence-curator`.

## Cinematic Rules

- Cutscene polish must not bypass runtime evidence.
- Palette swaps, fades and audio cues must have ownership and timing.
- Effects must serve narrative/gameplay, not exist as isolated decoration.
