# AAA Video Curation Handoff

Status: `candidate_applied_not_verified`

Use this handoff when `visual-excellence-standards` reviews Mega Drive screenshots, sprites, backgrounds, FX or claims of AAA visual quality.

## New Required Routes

- Route CRAM and vibrance claims to `palette-cram-curator`.
- Route dithering/composite transparency to `dither-composite-transparency`.
- Route Shadow/Highlight, line scroll and raster palette effects to their dedicated hardware skills.
- Route final approval to `emulator-vdp-evidence-curator` when runtime evidence is required.

## Review Rules

- Do not approve "vibrant" if the palette is muddy, desaturated or uninspected.
- Do not treat LCD/raw-pixel dithering as equivalent to CRT/composite blend.
- Do not approve visual spectacle unless it has a hardware plan and gameplay purpose.
- Do not let text reports replace visual evidence.
